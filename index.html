Built for real mapping flow.

PhysMap keeps the workflow simple: pick output, draw surfaces, align corners and add motion.

01 / Route

Choose output

Send a clean fullscreen canvas to the projector while the editor stays on your computer.

02 / Shape

Draw surfaces

Create squares, triangles, circles or free paths, then drag corners until light fits the physical object.

03 / Play

Add motion

Use Quick Looks, 41 animated fills, text, video, camera, YouTube, sound-reactive motion and particles.

Made for when the lights go down.

Projection mapping works when the output is clean and the editor stays usable. Everything else builds on that.

Projector safe

Black output

Outside mapped content, the output remains black so unwanted light stays off the scene.

Animation library

41 projection-ready animations, from Laser Fan and Circuit to Fire, Plasma and Galaxy, each with a live preview.

Live control

Freeze and blackout

Hold the frame or cut the projector to black without closing your scene.

Sound reactive

A microphone or an audio file drives any surface. Spectrum, Waveform and Beat Ring follow the music on their own.
